Our Story

Where heritage meets craftsmanship, and every piece tells a timeless story of passion, tradition, and artistry.

Our Journey Through Time

1985

Humble Beginnings

Zebkar began as a small workshop in the heart of the old city, where master craftsman Hamna Nadeem created his first collection of traditional jewelry pieces using techniques passed down through generations of his family.

1998

The First Boutique

After years of perfecting his craft, Ibrahim opened the first Zebkar boutique, bringing his exquisite creations to a wider audience while maintaining the intimate, personalized service that would become our hallmark.

2010

A New Generation

Ibrahim's daughter, Leila, joined the business, blending traditional techniques with contemporary design sensibilities. This fusion of old and new marked the beginning of Zebkar's modern era while honoring our rich heritage.

Present Day

Global Recognition

Today, Zebkar is celebrated internationally for our unique approach to jewelry making, with pieces gracing red carpets and royal collections worldwide, all while remaining true to our artisanal roots and commitment to craftsmanship.

Our Core Values

✦

Heritage Preservation

We are guardians of ancient jewelry-making techniques, preserving methods that have been perfected over centuries while adapting them for contemporary appreciation.

✦

Artisanal Excellence

Every Zebkar piece is created by hand, with each artisan investing not just skill but soul into their work. We celebrate the human touch in an age of mass production.

✦

Ethical Responsibility

From sourcing materials to working with artisans, we prioritize ethical practices, fair compensation, and sustainable methods at every stage of our process.

A Legacy in the Making

At Zebkar, we believe that true luxury is not about price tags but about connection. Each piece we create is a bridge between generations—a way to honor the past while creating new memories for the future.
